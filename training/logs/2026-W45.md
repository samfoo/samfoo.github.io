>

> back, just resume easy when you're home. If you want to keep the pattern alive, the Tier B bodyweight
> circuit (`strength.md`) once or twice is plenty. Expect the first week back on the bar to feel rusty,
> not weak — start a notch below the W40 test loads and climb.
