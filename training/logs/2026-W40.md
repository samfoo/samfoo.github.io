- **What worked / what to keep:** the 3-runs + 2-lifts spine with climbing as a real session, and
  RPE-capping strength at 8 in the deficit — strength went *up* across the board while bodyweight
  came *down*, which is the whole game. The deep Z2 base the running built carried straight over to
  the 100 km ride. Autoregulation (easing off on tired/travel weeks instead of forcing it) kept it
  sustainable for 13 weeks with no injury layoff.
- **Goals scorecard:** (1) **Body fat** — real progress but not all the way: 86.8→83.9 kg, ~23.4→~22%,
  waist 95→~91. Moving toward 16%, not there; call it ~⅓–½ of the target gap closed. (2) **Endurance/CV**
  — ✅ clearly: VO₂max 47→48, fitness age 40.2→39.2, RHR 55→53, *and* a 100 km century completed.
  (3) **Strength** — ✅ emphatically: squat +25, deadlift +20, OHP +15, bench +10–15 kg, all while
  leaning out. Gaining strength in a deficit is the standout win.
- **Where to next:** one loose end — the **5 km benchmark** got bumped by visiting friends, so **W41
  is a short bonus week to close it out** (plus travel: NY, run-only except Mon). After that: the body-fat
  goal is the unfinished one, so a sensible Block 4 is *continue the recomp* — hold this lifting frequency
  to protect the new strength, keep the easy aerobic base, and let the deficit keep trimming waist toward
  the mid-80s/16% with patience. Re-measure waist early W41 for the honest fat-loss read.
