# Strength program

Two full-body sessions per week — the optimal frequency for two lifting days, and it hits squat,
deadlift, and bench twice each over the week (squat pattern + bench on Day A, deadlift + press on
Day B). You're an experienced lifter, so form cues are minimal here; the structure and progression
are the point.

Everything is **RPE-capped at 8** because we're in a deficit (see [`principles.md`](principles.md)).
We keep intensity respectable (heavier, lower reps as the blocks progress) so strength is preserved,
with just enough volume to drive a little growth.

## Day A — Squat + Bench focus

| Order | Lift | Sets × reps | Notes |
|-------|------|-------------|-------|
| A1 | **Back squat** | per block (see table) | the priority lift — do it first, fresh |
| A2 | **Bench press** | per block | |
| A3 | Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8–10 @ RPE7 | posterior chain, primes Day B's deadlift |
| A4 | Chest-supported / DB row | 3 × 8–12 | pull volume for balance |
| A5 | Core — hanging leg raise or ab wheel | 3 × 8–12 | |

## Day B — Deadlift focus

| Order | Lift | Sets × reps | Notes |
|-------|------|-------------|-------|
| B1 | **Deadlift** | per block (see table) | priority lift — fresh. Fewer total sets than squat; it's taxing |
| B2 | **Overhead press** (or incline bench) | per block | vertical/inclined press; alternate with bench emphasis |
| B3 | Front squat or walking lunge | 3 × 8–10 | quad + second squat-pattern exposure |
| B4 | Pull-up (weighted if easy) | 3 × AMRAP-1 | keep 1 rep in reserve; great for climbing carryover |
| B5 | Core — plank / pallof press | 3 × 30–45 s | anti-rotation, protects the back |

Time-boxing to ~50 min: superset the accessories (A3+A4, B3+B4) with ~60–90 s rest. Take fuller
rest (2–3 min) on the main lifts only.

## Progression by block

Main lifts (squat, bench, deadlift, OHP) follow this. Deadlift uses the **lower** end of set counts
(it's the most fatiguing).

| Block | Main-lift sets × reps | Cap | How to add load (double progression) |
|-------|----------------------|-----|--------------------------------------|
| **1 · Foundation** (wk 1–3) | 3–4 × 6–8 | RPE 7→8 | when you hit the **top** of the rep range on all sets at ≤ RPE7, add **2.5 kg** (upper) / **5 kg** (lower) next session |
| **1 · Deload** (wk 4) | 2 × 5 | RPE 6 | ~85% of your working weight, easy |
| **2 · Build** (wk 5–7) | 4 × 4–6 | RPE 7→8 | same rule, hit top of 4–6 range then add load |
| **2 · Deload** (wk 8) | 2 × 5 | RPE 6 | light |
| **3 · Progress** (wk 9–12) | 4–5 × 3–5 | RPE 8 | hit top of 3–5 range then add load; small jumps only |
| **Test** (wk 13) | work up to a top set of 3 @ RPE8 | RPE 8 | record it — this is your re-test |

**Double progression in one sentence:** stay at a weight until you can do all prescribed sets at the
top of the rep range without exceeding the RPE cap, *then* add the smallest jump. In a deficit,
progress will be slow or flat on some lifts — **that is a success**, not a stall. Deadlift and squat
usually keep climbing; bench/OHP may just hold. Holding strength while losing fat = you're getting
relatively stronger every week.

## Autoregulation on the day

- Feeling flat (bad sleep, high RHR)? Drop to the bottom of the set range, or cut one set. Keep the
  weight, keep the movement, lose the junk volume.
- A lift feels heavier than last time at the same weight two sessions running → don't add load; hold
  or back off. Trust the RPE, not the plan's arrows.

## Travel — Tier B bodyweight session

No gym? This keeps the strength stimulus alive in a hotel room, ~25–35 min. Do it in place of a lift
day. Make it hard by slowing tempo and chasing higher reps / harder single-leg variants.

| Lift | Sets × reps | Swaps as it gets easy |
|------|-------------|-----------------------|
| Bulgarian split squat (rear foot on chair) | 3–4 × 10–15/leg | add a backpack of books; slow 3 s down |
| Push-up (feet elevated / decline) | 3–4 × AMRAP-2 | archer / diamond / deficit push-ups |
| Inverted row (under a sturdy table) or backpack row | 3–4 × AMRAP-2 | feet elevated; one-arm backpack row |
| Single-leg RDL (bodyweight or backpack) | 3 × 10/leg | slow eccentric; loaded |
| Pike push-up (shoulders) | 3 × 8–12 | elevate feet toward handstand |
| Plank + side plank | 3 × 30–45 s | add reach / leg lift |

"Greasing the groove" also works well on nothing-days: a few sub-maximal sets of push-ups /
squats / a pull-up if there's a bar, spread through the day.
