Friedhats — Las Margaritas Niche @ 37 20g : 300g (1:15). 50g bloom, 30s @ 62°C. +150g @ 92°C. +100g @ 92°C. Pour steps much cleaner this time. Acidity basically gone. Chocolatey, vaguely sweet. Whole brew runs long enough that it's not properly warm by the end.
Friedhats — Las Margaritas Niche @ 38 20g : 300g (1:15). 100g bloom, 30s @ 62°C. +100g over 1:00 @ 92°C, then the same again. Much less acidic than @ 42 — decent cup. Big bloom was an accident (botched the pour), not a deliberate change.
Friedhats — Las Margaritas Niche @ 42 20g : 300g (1:15). 50g bloom, 30s. +150g over 1:00. +100g over ~0:40. A bit sour. Not the best pour. Need to work on bloom technique. Grind possibly slightly too coarse — try finer next time.
